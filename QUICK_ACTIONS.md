## 📸 Add a Profile Picture (Recommended)

1. Go to https://github.com/settings/profile
2. Click on your current avatar
3. Upload a professional photo or create one:
   - Use [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) for design
   - Or use [UI Avatars](https://ui-avatars.com/) for text-based avatar
   - Recommended size: 400x400px
